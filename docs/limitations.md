# Limitations

- `year_built` is the assessor's structure construction year, not a subdivision date.
- Current parcel boundaries are not original historic lots.
- Parcel splits and consolidations require historical parcel layers, plats, deeds, or tax maps.
- Multiple structures on one PIN require primary-building selection logic.
- Some assessor year-built values may be missing, stale, approximate, or incorrect.
- Replacement structures can make a parcel look newer than its subdivision or neighborhood context.
- Historical aerial imagery may need georeferencing before accurate overlay.
- Sanborn maps and historic plats may have access, licensing, or digitization constraints.
- Municipality filtering can have edge cases along municipal borders.
- V1 omits owner names and keeps the product focused on property history and urban development.
