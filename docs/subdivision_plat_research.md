# Subdivision Plat Research

Subdivision history should be grounded in recorded plat evidence.

The future `cook_recorded_subdivision_plats` layer is reserved for lots created by recorded subdivision plats. Good records should include:

- Subdivision name.
- Recording date.
- Document number or book/page reference.
- Lot and block numbers.
- Legal description.
- Source office or archive.
- Any georeferencing or digitizing notes.

Assessor year-built data can help explain building activity after subdivision, but it should not be used as the subdivision date.
